Privacy

Your information matters.

A short explanation of how enquiries are handled on malie.me.

What this website collects

The consultation form does not send or store your details on a website server. It opens your email application with a draft enquiry. Information is shared only when you choose to send that email.

What you may send

Enquiries may include your name, email address, country or time zone, preferred coaching pathway and English-learning goals. Please do not send confidential patient records or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

How enquiries are used

Information you email is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss coaching suitability and, if you decide to proceed, communicate about your coaching arrangements. It is not sold to advertisers.

Your choices

You may ask about information you have sent or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations, by emailing hello@malie.me.

External services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your email application and email provider handle messages you choose to send. Their respective privacy policies also apply.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@malie.me.
