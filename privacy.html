Privacy notice

Your information matters.

How enquiries and personal information are handled when you contact malie.

Clear, considered, personal.

This notice explains how I handle the limited information involved when you enquire about English coaching or book a consultation.

What this website collects

The consultation form does not send or store your details on a website server. It prepares an email draft using your own email application. Information is shared only when you send the email.

What you may send

Enquiries may contain your name, email address, country or time zone, preferred programme and communication goals. Please avoid patient records, medical histories or other unnecessary sensitive personal information.

How your information is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss whether coaching suits your needs and communicate about coaching arrangements if you proceed. It is not sold to advertisers.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction or deletion of information you sent, subject to applicable legal obligations, by emailing hello@malie.me.

Who handles enquiries?

Malie Brown, trading online as malie, handles coaching enquiries. You can contact me about privacy and your personal information at hello@malie.me.

How long is information kept?

Enquiry correspondence is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and manage any resulting coaching relationship, or longer where law requires. You may ask about the information held and request correction, objection or deletion where applicable.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently install its own analytics or advertising cookies. It uses external Google Fonts and GitHub Pages hosting, which may process technical connection information. TidyCal operates separately when you follow a booking link and may use cookies on its own website. Please consult those providers’ notices for their practices.

External services and international processing

GitHub Pages hosts this website, Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, and TidyCal processes online consultation bookings. Email services handle messages you choose to send. Some of these providers operate outside South Africa, so information may be processed internationally under their respective terms and privacy policies. This website does not operate an advertising tracker.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@malie.me. Last updated November 2026.
