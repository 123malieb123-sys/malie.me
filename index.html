About Me

Hello, I’m Malie Brown — a CELTA-certified Online English Tutor with over 7 years of experience helping learners build confidence and fluency in English.

I specialise in General English, Business English, IELTS/Cambridge preparation, and young learner instruction. My lessons are practical, supportive, and tailored to each student’s goals.

My teaching focuses on real-world communication, pronunciation, fluency, and confidence. I also bring experience in business, administration, recruitment, and client communication, which helps me support adult learners and professionals.

I am currently studying toward a BCom in Project Management and am also a STEM.org Certified™ Master Trainer.
