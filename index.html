About Me

Hello, I’m Malie Brown — a CELTA-certified Online English Tutor with over 7 years of experience helping learners worldwide build confidence and fluency in English.

I specialise in General English, Business English, IELTS/Cambridge preparation, and young learner instruction, creating lessons that are practical, engaging, and tailored to each student’s goals.

My teaching approach focuses on real-world communication, pronunciation, fluency, and learner confidence. I believe students learn best in supportive environments where lessons are interactive, structured, and adapted to individual learning styles.

Alongside teaching, I am currently studying toward a BCom in Project Management and am also a STEM.org Certified™ Master Trainer, allowing me to integrate creativity, critical thinking, and digital learning tools into my lessons.

Before moving into education, I gained professional experience in insurance, administration, recruitment, and client communication roles. This background helps me support adult learners and professionals preparing for interviews, presentations, workplace communication, and international opportunities.

I am passionate about helping students communicate naturally, grow in confidence, and achieve meaningful progress in English.
