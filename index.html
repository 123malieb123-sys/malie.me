About Me

Rooted in Heritage.
Growing Every Mind.

My name is Malie, and I have spent over a decade standing at the front of classrooms, sitting at kitchen tables, and walking alongside students through some of the most pivotal chapters of their lives. Teaching, to me, is not a profession — it is a calling shaped by the proverb that gave this website its heartbeat:

"It takes a village to raise a child — and I am proud to be part of that village."

Born from a deep love of learning and community, my approach blends rigorous academic support with emotional intelligence, cultural affirmation, and genuine human connection. I meet every student where they are, and walk with them toward where they are capable of going — further than they ever imagined.

Whether I am tutoring a struggling learner, mentoring a young leader, or consulting with families navigating the education system, I bring the same warmth, expertise, and unwavering belief that every child deserves to thrive.
