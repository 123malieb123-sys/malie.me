About this website

malie.me is the English coaching website of Malie Brown, based in South Africa. It describes available coaching pathways and offers ways to enquire or request a consultation.

Information about programmes

Programme descriptions are general information, not a binding offer or guarantee of availability. Coaching content, schedule, fees, payment arrangements, cancellation conditions and any other relevant terms will be confirmed separately before you agree to paid coaching.

Free consultations

The introductory 20-minute OET programme consultation is offered without a booking fee or obligation to enrol. It is an introductory discussion, not a free English lesson or full OET assessment.

Language coaching and outcomes

Coaching supports English communication and language development. It does not provide clinical training, medical advice or a guarantee of a particular examination or career outcome. The OET name refers to an external language assessment; this website does not claim official OET affiliation or endorsement.
