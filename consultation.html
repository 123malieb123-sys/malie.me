Good to know

A few useful details.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The 20-minute introductory consultation is free and there is no obligation to enrol.

How is coaching delivered?

Sessions take place online, so we can work together across locations and time zones.

Do I need to know my English level?

No. We start by discussing your goals and what you need to do in English.

How do we arrange a time?

Send your enquiry, including your country or time zone. We can then agree on a suitable time.

Do you guarantee OET results?

No. Coaching supports English language development, practice and feedback; examination outcomes cannot be guaranteed.
