Common questions

Good to know.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The 20-minute introductory consultation is free and there is no obligation to enrol.

Where does coaching take place?

Coaching is delivered online, so we can work together from different locations and time zones.

Do I need to know my English level?

No. We can discuss your current communication needs and determine an appropriate starting point.

Do you guarantee examination results?

No. Coaching focuses on language development, targeted practice and feedback. Examination outcomes cannot be guaranteed.

How do I choose a time?

Send your enquiry with your country or time zone and a little information about your goals. We can then agree on a suitable time.
