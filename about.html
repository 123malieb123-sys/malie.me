Hi, I’m Malie. I help adults develop the clarity, language and confidence to communicate effectively at work, in healthcare and in everyday life.

You’re not a level or a list of mistakes. You’re a person with something to say.

I bring more than eight years of English teaching experience to individual learners and professional groups, with experience creating learning materials and programmes for real communication.
