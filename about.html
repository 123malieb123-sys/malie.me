Hi, I'm Malie.
It's good to meet you.

I'm an English teacher and coach who believes the most valuable language learning happens when it connects to your real life.

Experienced educator · Individual attentionSouth Africa · Worldwide online

My approach

You are not a level, a textbook or a list of mistakes. You're a person with something to say.

I work with learners as individuals. We identify what's getting in the way, practise the communication that matters and adjust the plan as your confidence and ability grow.

Whether you are preparing for an important professional conversation or simply want to feel more comfortable speaking, my aim is to help you use English with greater clarity and independence.

What working together feels like

Practical. Personal. Purposeful.

Sessions are built around meaningful communication rather than endless worksheets. We work on what you need, make room for real practice and use feedback to help you notice the difference.

  1. Learning goals that make sense for your life.
  2. Respect for your experience and existing skills.
  3. Supportive, specific feedback.
  4. Practice you can use beyond the lesson.
